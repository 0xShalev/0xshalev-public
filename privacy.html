Brain privacy policy

Brain is a private, self-hosted personal assistant used by one household.

Google data Brain accesses

  • Your Google account email address, to show which account is connected.
  • Your calendar events and calendar list, to show your schedule, remind you, and add, change or delete events.

Storage

The access token and any calendar data Brain keeps are stored on the household's own server. Event details can also appear in your chat history, reminders and notes you ask Brain to save, including the owner's private backups of those notes.

Sharing

When you ask the assistant something that needs your calendar, the relevant event data is sent to Anthropic (the Claude API) to produce the answer. Notifications are delivered through your browser's push service, or a notification service run by the owner. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by Brain to train AI models.

Brain's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Disconnect on Brain's Setup page, which revokes and deletes the token, or remove Brain at your Google account permissions. Ask the owner to delete stored data.
